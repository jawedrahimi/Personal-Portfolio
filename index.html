I like engineering projects that move from theory to working systems.

I’m an Electrical Engineering & Computer Sciences student at the University of California, Berkeley with experience across electrical systems, software engineering, digital design, distributed systems, robotics, autonomy, artificial intelligence, and space systems engineering.

I’m especially interested in hands-on engineering where software and hardware come together. I enjoy working with processors, circuits, sensors, embedded hardware, automation, real-time systems, power architectures, and robotic systems.
