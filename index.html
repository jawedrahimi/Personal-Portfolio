INTRODUCTION

About Me

Hi, I'm Ahmad, an Electrical Engineering & Computer Sciences student at the University of California, Berkeley. My interests include electrical systems, software engineering, embedded systems, robotics, autonomy, artificial intelligence, and space systems engineering.

What excites me most about engineering is taking an idea from theory into something that can actually be built, tested, measured, debugged, and improved.

I enjoy working through the complete engineering process: requirements → design → analysis → implementation → testing → iteration.

My experience includes electrical and power subsystem work for a lunar rover mission through NASA L’SPACE, automation and hydroponic engineering with Vertical Farming at Berkeley, electrical systems work with UC Berkeley Formula Racing, and software development using Python, C++, Java, Ruby, and web technologies.

I am especially enthusiastic about hands-on projects where software, electronics, sensors, autonomy, and physical systems come together to solve real-world engineering problems.
